COOL Autonomy Lab. Together we’re COOLer.

    The Control, Optimization, and Online Learning (COOL) for Autonomy Lab is based at the University of Texas at Austin. Our mission is to develop advanced real-time decision-making methods that enable autonomous systems to complement humans in performing complex tasks. We are committed to educating and training the next generation of scientists, researchers, and engineers in this rapidly advancing field. We value teamwork, hard work, and having fun, because together we are COOLer.
